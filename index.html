About Us

Your Reliable Chemical Procurement Partner

Gunjal Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted name in India's chemical industry, operating from GIDC Ankleshwar — the heart of India's chemical corridor. We manufacture and resell Organic & Inorganic Chemicals, Pharma Intermediates, and Speciality Chemicals.

Our expertise in regulatory documentation enables clients in compliance-heavy markets worldwide to operate with full confidence. Competitive pricing, quality assurance, and on-time delivery are our core commitments.
